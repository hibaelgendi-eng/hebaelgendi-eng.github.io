The problem · المشكلة

Wajz had strong services.
But the value wasn't immediately clear.

The previous experience presented services as separate offerings, without a strong narrative explaining how they work together or why that matters to the client.

The insight

The opportunity wasn't to look more technical.
It was to become more memorable.

If everyone is speaking the language of technology, differentiation can come from the experience and the story — not from adding more technology visuals.

Design decisions · قرارات التصميم

Every decision had a reason.

01

Dark theme first

Why?

Reflects the brand's premium digital character and creates stronger contrast for the Wajz visual language.

02

Gold for CTAs

Why?

Use the strongest accent where attention and action matter most.

03

Services as a system

Why?

Position services as connected parts of one ecosystem rather than isolated offerings.

04

Mobile-first thinking

Why?

The mobile experience should feel designed for the context, not simply reduced from desktop.
